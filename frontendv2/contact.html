Downpayment & appointment policies

Downpayment to secure your slot
  • Soft gel extensions: ₱300.
  • Gel manicure: ₱200.
  • The downpayment is deducted from your total and is non-refundable.
  • Pay through the GCash account provided by the owner, then send a screenshot of your proof of payment.
  • No downpayment, no secured slot. Wait for confirmation that your payment and appointment slot are confirmed.
Late arrivals & cancellations
  • A 1–15 minute delay incurs an additional ₱150 charge.
  • Arriving more than 15 minutes late means the appointment is cancelled.
  • There are no cancellations of secured slots. If you cancel, your downpayment is forfeited.
  • Please make sure your chosen time works for you before confirming.
Rescheduling: notice and replacement date
  • How early you request: Request at least 48 hours before your reserved slot to keep your downpayment; a ₱200 rescheduling fee applies. If you request with less than 48 hours’ notice, a separate downpayment is required to secure the new slot.
  • How far out the new date is: The replacement date must be within 7 days of the original appointment. If it is beyond 7 days, the original downpayment is forfeited and a new downpayment is required.
  • Message your preferred new date and time so availability can be discussed.
